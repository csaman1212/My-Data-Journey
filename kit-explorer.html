    Add an assembly revision from Epicor

    1. In Method Tracker click the assembly (the kit, a box, or a part with a little arrow). The Revision tab shows its Rev and Effective date. Then open Materials → List.
    2. Right-click the grid and choose Copy All Include Labels.
    3. Fill in the fields below, paste into the box, and press Save revision. Save each revision of a kit separately to compare them.

    Saved revisions

    A part listed inside one assembly is linked automatically to the assembly of the same part number you saved. The effective dates decide which revision of a sub-assembly belongs to which revision of its parent. A revision is used until the next revision's effective date.
