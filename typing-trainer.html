How this works

Type the text shown as it streams by. Correct characters turn light, mistakes are highlighted red.

  • Adaptive Lesson: starts with a couple of keys and unlocks new ones automatically once you're fast and accurate on the current set.
  • Weakness Drill: click letters on the keyboard to build words concentrated on the keys you want to improve. Leave nothing selected and it auto-targets your weakest keys.
  • Key colors in the "All keys" row: gray = not introduced, blue = new, yellow = still learning, red = weak, green = mastered.
  • The keyboard below only highlights the exact key you should press next; it doesn't repeat the mastery colors shown above.
  • The progress bar under "Learning rate" shows how close your current key is to unlocking the next one.
  • Every few unlocked keys, a review lesson mixes everything you've learned so far and tells you afterward if anything needs re-practice.
  • Once enough real English words can be built from your unlocked keys, lessons switch from letter drills to complete words automatically.
  • The "Keyboard reference" checkbox below the keyboard hides it to force memorization; by default it auto-hides after 3 lessons (adjustable, or turn it off, in Settings). Check the box anytime to bring it back.
