    Add an assembly from Epicor

    1. In Method Tracker click the assembly (the kit, a box, or a part with a little arrow) and open Materials → List.
    2. Right-click the grid and choose Copy All Include Labels.
    3. Type the assembly's part number below, paste into the box, and press Save assembly.

    Saved assemblies

    A part listed inside one assembly is linked automatically to the assembly of the same part number you saved. That is how sub-parts appear under their parent.

    Automatic backup to your OneDrive folder

    Your data is always saved in this browser. Connect a folder that lives inside OneDrive and every change is also written there as one file (kit-explorer-backup.json), including your pictures. OneDrive then syncs it. Works in Chrome and Edge.

    Works in every browser. Download a copy, or restore from one. Restoring replaces what is currently in this browser.
